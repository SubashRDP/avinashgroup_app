# 11. Glossary

[Back to contents](README.md)

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **AD** | The English (Gregorian) calendar. ERPNext stores dates in AD |
| **Additional Salary** | A one-month amount for one person (earning or deduction). Made by Prepare Payroll Inputs, Payroll Adjustment, Salary Revision (arrears) and advance recovery |
| **Allowance Kind** | How an allowance is paid: Fixed per Person, Fixed Company Rate, % of Initial Basic, Per Day Present, Per Meal, Per Hour (Overtime), Entered by Hand, Yearly (Chapter 2) |
| **Allowances table** | The table on a person's Salary Structure Assignment listing the allowances they get, with their own amount or the company rate |
| **Arrears** | The difference owed for months already paid at the old rate when a raise is agreed late. Paid as **Salary Arrears** in one month |
| **Attendance** | One record per person per day: Present, Absent, Half Day, On Leave, Work From Home |
| **Attendance Device ID** | The number a person is enrolled under on the biometric device |
| **Attendance Fix** | A screen that rebuilds attendance from the punches for a shift or a list of people over a date range |
| **Basic** | The person's monthly basic salary, the **Base** on their Salary Structure Assignment |
| **BS (Bikram Sambat)** | The Nepali calendar. The books and payroll run on BS months |
| **BS Month** | A Nepali month: Shrawan, Bhadra, Ashwin, Kartik, Mangsir, Poush, Magh, Falgun, Chaitra, Baisakh, Jestha, Ashadh |
| **CIT** | Citizen Investment Trust contribution; reduces taxable income |
| **Company rate / Default Rate** | The company's standard rate for an allowance (tea per day, meal per meal, gas per month, HRA %), on Salary Component → Accounts row → Default Rate |
| **Daily Breakdown** | The table on every salary slip that shows each day's attendance, pay and fines, adding up to the net pay |
| **DA** | Dearness Allowance, a person's own fixed monthly allowance |
| **Employee Category** | **Operation** (paid overtime) or **Officer & Admin** (replacement leave). Decides what holiday and extra work earns |
| **F/P** | Filling Plant. The **Plant** payroll section, and its salary expense account |
| **Fiscal Year** | Shrawan 1 to the end of Ashadh. "83/84" = Shrawan 2083 to Ashadh 2084 |
| **Gross** | Total earnings on the slip, before deductions |
| **Half Day** | Half the day worked. More than 2 hours late, or coming in while on leave, also makes a Half Day |
| **HRA** | House Rent Allowance: a percentage (NGI 25%, NGN 27%) of the person's Initial Basic |
| **Initial Basic** | A person's starting basic, kept on their assignment. HRA is worked out from it, not from the current Basic |
| **IRD** | Inland Revenue Department, where salary tax (SST and remuneration tax) is deposited |
| **Late Excused** | A tick on one day's Attendance that stops the late fine for that day (with a reason) |
| **Late Fine** | A deduction for minutes late in and minutes left early: Basic ÷ 30 ÷ 8 per hour |
| **Late Fine Exempt** | A tick on the Employee for staff never fined for lateness |
| **Late Time** | Minutes late in + minutes left early, on a full working day |
| **Leave Without Pay (LWP)** | Unpaid leave. The day is not paid |
| **Matches Journal** | The green card on the Avinas Salary Statement: the books agree with the salary sheet |
| **Meal** | Paid for authorised overtime: 1 for coming 1.5 h early, 1 for staying 1.5 h late, 2 for a holiday on the sheet; at most 2 a day |
| **Miti** | A BS date, for example "03 Shrawan" or "2083-04-03" |
| **Month calendar** | The 12-month picker on the Payroll Entry form where you click the month to pay |
| **Nepal BS Period** | Optional screen to change a month's first and last day for payroll |
| **Net Pay / Net Payable** | What the employee receives: gross minus deductions |
| **O/O** | Office. The **Admin & Accounts** payroll section, and its salary expense account |
| **OT** | Overtime. Paid at Basic ÷ 30 ÷ 8 × 1.5 per hour, only on an approved Overtime Sheet |
| **OT Eligible** | A tick on the Employee, filled from the Employee Category. Only OT-eligible staff get overtime and meals |
| **Overtime Sheet** | The document that authorises extra work for a date. Without it, no overtime or meal is paid |
| **Payment Days** | Days paid in the month. Holidays are paid; absent and unpaid days are not |
| **Payroll Adjustment** | A screen to enter one-off amounts for many people in one month |
| **Payroll Entry** | The document for one company's payroll for one BS month |
| **Payroll Payable Account** | The liability account for salary owed: 347301 Salary Payable. Must have no Account Type |
| **Payroll Section** | Admin & Accounts (O/O), Marketing (S/D) or Plant (F/P), set on each Department |
| **Prepare Payroll Inputs** | The button on the Payroll Entry that works out tea, meals, overtime, late fines and advance instalments |
| **Regular Salary** | On the salary sheet: Net Basic + SSF Addition + Total Allowance |
| **Replacement Leave** | A day of leave (compensatory leave) earned by Officer & Admin staff for working a holiday on an Overtime Sheet |
| **S/D** | Sales and Distribution. The **Marketing** payroll section, and its salary expense account |
| **Salary Revision** | The screen for pay raises: makes new dated assignments, carries allowances over, pays arrears |
| **Salary Slip BS** | The print format of the payslip in Bikram Sambat, with the Daily Breakdown on page 2. Also what is emailed |
| **Salary Structure** | What everyone in a company's payroll shares: Basic, SSF Addition, SSF, Income Tax and the tags (for example **NGI Staff 83/84**) |
| **Salary Structure Assignment** | A person's pay from a date: Base (Basic), Initial Basic, SSF Applicable, Allowances, tax slab, payable account |
| **Sandwich rule** | Holidays that fall between two leave days are counted as leave |
| **SSF** | Social Security Fund. On the slip, **SSF** is the deduction of **31% of Basic** (20% employer + 11% employee), deposited by the 15th of the next month |
| **SSF Addition** | The employer's **20%** share, added to the employee's gross as an earning (so the employee's own cost is 11%) |
| **SSF Applicable** | A tick on the assignment. Unticked: no SSF, and the person pays 1% SST instead |
| **SST** | Social Security Tax: the 1% first band of income tax, paid only by staff **not** in SSF |
| **Tag** | A tea, meal, overtime or late fine line in a Salary Structure. It means "applies to everyone on this structure"; the amount comes from attendance |
| **Tea & Conveyance** | Paid per day present on working days (not on holidays); NGI 235, NGN 265 per day, or the person's own rate |
| **TDS** | Tax deducted at source. Salary TDS is booked to TDS-Remuneration |
| **Worked on Holiday** | A tick on Attendance when the day is a holiday on the person's own list |
