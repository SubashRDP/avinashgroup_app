# Holiday lists

## The shape

Two Holiday Lists per company per fiscal year — 14 for FY 83/84:

| List | Who follows it | Set on |
|---|---|---|
| `<ABBR> 83/84` | everyone | Company → Default Holiday List |
| `<ABBR> 83/84 (Women)` | female employees | Employee → Holiday List |

The women's list is a superset: every common day, plus the women-only days.
ERPNext has no per-gender holiday, and every engine that asks "is this date a
holiday" (salary slip payment days, leave day counting, auto attendance, our
allowance engine and BS reports) resolves the employee's own list first and the
company default second. Two lists is therefore the only way that needs no patch
to HRMS.

FY 83/84 lists run 2026-07-17 → 2027-07-16 and hold every Saturday plus the
festivals. The women's lists add Haritalika Teej (2026-09-14, **date still to be
confirmed against the government notice**) and International Women's Day
(2027-03-08).

Decisions behind this: `docs/hr-decisions/00-policy-minutes-2026-09-15.txt`
(2.1 each company may differ, 2.4 Teej is women-only and there are no optional
holidays).

## Changing them — Holiday Bulk Update

A mid-year government holiday would otherwise be typed into fourteen lists by
hand, and the list that gets missed is discovered in payroll, when everyone on it
is Absent on a day the rest of the group had off.

**Holiday Bulk Update** (Avinash Group App) does the whole set at once:

1. **Action** — Add Holiday or Remove Holiday.
2. **Companies** — all, or pick them.
3. **Holidays table** — one row per date: Date, Holiday, and `Women Only` for a
   row that belongs in the women's copy alone.
4. **Apply** — reports, per list, what changed and what was skipped.

Skipped means: the date is outside that list's range, already present when
adding, absent when removing, or a weekly off (Saturdays are generated by the
Holiday List's own button and are never removed one date at a time).

Code: `avinashgroup_app/hr/holiday_lists.py`.

## After a past date changes

Attendance already marked is not touched. Re-run those days through **Attendance
Fix** so the day becomes a holiday for the people who now have it.
