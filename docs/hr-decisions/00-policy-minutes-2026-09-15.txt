HRMS & PAYROLL POLICY - CLIENT DECISIONS

Scope: Attendance, Holiday, Leave, Shift, Overtime, Salary Components, Admin

These are DECISIONS. They overrule the defaults in 01-leave-type.txt and
02-holiday-list.txt. The right-hand notes say where each one is configured.


1. ATTENDANCE & TIMING
   1.1 No grace period. May be kept flexible where the situation demands.
       -> Shift Type: late_entry_grace_period = 0. Keep the grace field usable
          so admin can relax it.
   1.2 More than 2 hours late = half day.
       -> per-shift rule; stock HRMS has no "late by X = half day", so this is
          ours to implement (Shift Type has working-hours thresholds only).
   1.3 Meal-eligible staff: 1 meal for coming 1.5 h early, 1 meal for staying
       1.5 h late. Admin-verified. Max 2 meals/day.
       -> allowance engine: Salary Component, Per Day, custom_time_offset_hours
          = 1.5, cap 2/day.
   1.4 Tea & conveyance for everyone present in the office.
       -> Per Day component on Present days. Rate per employee (plant vs office).
   1.5 Late-hour deduction is OPTIONAL, so it can be adjusted.
       -> keep the late-fine component switchable; do not hard-wire it.

2. HOLIDAY
   2.1 Each company may keep a DIFFERENT holiday calendar.
       -> one Holiday List per company, not one shared list.
   2.2 CORRECTED 2026-09-16 (the minute as circulated says the opposite):
       Admin and Officer level DO get replacement leave for working on a
       holiday. Staff BELOW admin/officer level do NOT — they are the
       OT-eligible ones and are paid overtime instead.
       -> one flag decides both: Employee.custom_ot_eligibility.
            OT eligible = 1 -> paid OT + meals, no comp-off
            OT eligible = 0 (admin/officer) -> comp-off, no OT
       -> same split as 2.5, which already reads that way.
   2.3 OT-eligible on a holiday: 1 meal at 6 hours, +1 meal at 8+ hours.
       Max 2/day.
   2.4 Teej is an EXTRA holiday for WOMEN ONLY. No other optional holidays.
       -> women's Holiday List = common list + Teej.
       -> NO optional-leave holiday list. Drop the one made on 2026-09-15
          ("Optional Holidays 83/84": English New Year + the two Eids) and put
          Eid back in the common list if the company gives it.
   2.5 Saturday work comes in TWO kinds:
         a) compensatory leave (staff who are not OT-eligible = admin/officer)
         b) OT paid (OT-eligible staff, no compensatory leave)
       -> the same single flag as 2.2.

3. LEAVE
   3.1 Types: Sick, Casual, Paternity, Maternity.
   3.2 33 days a year, credited 2.75 a month.
       -> Leave Type: Is Earned Leave, Monthly, Rounding 0.25.
          Leave Policy: 33.
   3.3 Admin may adjust the monthly credit, as long as the year stays within
       33 days.
       -> Leave Type max_leaves_allowed = 33 as the ceiling; manual top-ups
          allowed below it.
   3.4 Coming to the office while on leave = half day.
   3.5 SANDWICH: a holiday between two leave days IS counted as leave.
       -> Leave Type: "Include holidays within leaves as leaves" = ON.
   3.6 Probation staff: 1 day a month.
       -> second Leave Policy (12/year), assigned while on probation.
   3.7 Leave left at the end of the fiscal year is ENCASHED.
       -> Leave Type: Allow Encashment ON + Earning Component.
          Carry forward therefore OFF (encash, don't carry).

4. SHIFT
   4.1 The Sales shift can be changed.
       -> keep dated Shift Assignments so a change keeps its history.

5. OVERTIME
   5.1 OT is tracked on a PER-DAY sheet, and that sheet decides eligibility.
   5.2 Max 2 meals a day for OT-eligible staff.

6. SALARY COMPONENTS
   6.1 The components themselves will NOT change. Only rates change.
   6.2 Teej gifts and other gifts do not touch salary components.
   6.3 Contribution: 11% employee / 20% company.
       *** OPEN: is this PF or SSF? ***
       (Their Falgun sheet shows SSF 31% deduction and 20% company addition,
        which is 11 + 20. NGG's sheet says "20% PF, CIT & SST" and NGK's says
        "10% PF Cont.", so it may differ per company.)

7. GENERAL & ADMIN
   7.1 Errors and mistakes are reported by message or email.
   7.2 Admin must be able to switch system options on and off, encashment
       included.
       -> put the switches somewhere admin can reach (settings doctype),
          not in code.
