How to open any screen

Every screen in this guide is opened the same way.

  1. Click the search bar at the very top of the page (it says Search or type a command).
  2. Type the screen name written in blue bold in this guide — for example Payroll Entry.
  3. Press Enter. You will see a list. To make a new one, click + Add on the right.
Tip

Buttons look like this in the guide. Boxes you type into look like this.

Save, Submit, Cancel — what they mean

ButtonWhat happensCan I change it later?
SaveKeeps your work as a Draft. Nothing is paid or counted yet.Yes, freely.
SubmitMakes it final. Now it counts — in salary, leave, reports.No. You must Cancel it.
CancelTakes back a submitted paper. The system undoes what it did.Then click Amend to make a corrected copy.

Save = राखिराख्ने (ड्राफ्ट) · Submit = पक्का गर्ने · Cancel = रद्द गर्ने

Money rules at a glance

The system does these sums for you. They are here so you can check a slip by hand.

Late fine

Attendance rules

  • More than 2 hours late after your shift starts = Half Day. No grace time.
  • Came to work while on approved leave = Half Day.
  • A half day still gets the full tea for that day.
  • A half day is not fined for being late — the half day is already the penalty.

Part B — Everyday and monthly work

Once setup is done, this is all the HR person does.

Attendance every day

The fingerprint machine makes attendance by itself. Your job is to check it and fix mistakes.

हाजिरी मेसिनले आफैं बनाउँछ। तपाईंले जाँच्ने र गल्ती सच्याउने मात्र हो।

Check yesterday

  1. Open Monthly Attendance BS.
  2. Choose your Company, Fiscal Year and BS Month. Leave View on Detail.
  3. Look for red flags: Not Marked, Absent for someone you saw at work, or an IN time with no OUT time.

Someone forgot to punch

  1. Open Attendance and find that person and date.
  2. If it is a Draft, change the Status and times, then Submit. If it is already submitted, click Cancel, then Amend, fix it, and Submit.
  3. Write the reason in the remarks — for example “machine down, confirmed by supervisor”.
Careful

Do not mark someone Present to be kind. Tea, meals, overtime and pay all follow this line.

Shifts when needed

Give someone their normal shift

  1. Open Shift Assignment Tool.
  2. Pick the Company, the Shift Type (for example 6 AM - 2 PM) and the Start Date. Leave End Date empty.
  3. Tick the employees and click Assign Shift.

Someone changes shift

  1. Open Shift Request and click + Add.
  2. Choose the employee, the new Shift Type and the From Date.
  3. For one day or a few days, fill To Date. The old shift comes back by itself after that.
    For a permanent move, leave To Date empty.
  4. Set status to Approved and Submit.
Why this matters

Late time, overtime and meals are all measured against the shift of that day. 09:08 is 8 minutes late on the 9 AM shift, but early on the 12 PM shift.

Leave when asked

There are only four kinds of paid leave. Anything else is unpaid.

LeaveWhoNote
Casual LeaveEveryoneKarnali (NGK): 8 a year. Others: 21.
Sick LeaveEveryone12 a year.
Maternity LeaveWomen onlyWhen a child is born. Not paid out in cash.
Paternity LeaveMen onlyWhen a child is born. Not paid out in cash.

Leave grows by 2.75 days every month (1 a month on probation). You do not add it — the system does, at the start of each BS month.

Enter a leave

  1. Open Leave Application → + Add.
  2. Choose employee, Leave Type, From Date, To Date. Tick Half Day if needed.
  3. Set Status to Approved and Submit.
Tip

To see how much leave someone has left, open the report Yearly Leave Details BS.

Overtime sheet on the day

Only hours written on a submitted Overtime Sheet are paid. No sheet, no overtime money.

ओभरटाइम सिटमा नभएको घण्टाको पैसा आउँदैन।

  1. Open Overtime Sheet → + Add.
  2. Fill Company, Date and Why Extra Work Was Needed (for example “gas loading backlog”).
  3. Add each person in the Employees table. The system shows whether they get Overtime or Replacement Leave.
  4. Submit the sheet.
  5. After the day is over, open the sheet again and click Measure from Attendance. The system reads the punches and fills Worked Hours (measured).
How hours are counted

Working day: only hours before the shift starts and after it ends. Holiday: every hour worked. Rounded to the nearest half hour — 7:53 becomes 8, 7:34 becomes 7.5.

Giving an advance when asked

Record it once. The system takes the instalment from salary every month until it is paid back.

  1. Open Employee Advance → + Add.
  2. Fill employee, Advance Amount and Purpose.
  3. Keep Repay Unclaimed Amount from Salary ticked. Type the Monthly Instalment (for example 2,500). If deduction should start later, fill Recover From.
  4. Submit.
  5. When the cash is handed over, click Make → Bank Entry (or Cash), choose the JV Type, and Submit the voucher.

Example: 10,000 at 2,500 a month → Bhadra 7,500 left → Ashwin 5,000 → … → status becomes Returned.

Careful

An advance that was never paid out (step 5) is never deducted. The system only takes back money it knows was given.

A new employee joins

Do these in order. If one is missed, the person will be missing from salary.

  1. Employee — name, Gender, Date of Joining, Company, Department, Designation.
  2. On the same Employee, in Payroll Inputs: Employee Category, Allowance Category (tea group), SSF Applicable, and their Dearness / Other / Fixed allowance amounts. Tick HRA, Gas or Education if they get it. Tick Late Fine Exempt only for managers.
  3. Shift Assignment Tool — give them a shift.
  4. Leave Policy Assignment — give them their company's leave policy (probation policy if on probation).
  5. Salary Structure Assignment — choose the company structure (for example NGN Staff 83/84), From Date = first day of the BS month, Base = their Basic. Labourers use the Daily Wage structure with Base = day rate. Submit.

Month-end salary every month

The most important page. Tick each box as you finish — the ticks stay on your computer.

Month-end checklist

Changed something after step 5?

Click Prepare Payroll Inputs again — it replaces what it made before, it never adds twice. If the slips are already made, open each changed person's slip and click Save so it picks up the new figures.

One-off amounts

For things that happen only this month for some people — an extra deduction, an extra payment.

  1. Open Payroll Adjustment → + Add.
  2. Fill Company and a Payroll Date inside the BS month.
  3. Add one row per person: employee, Component (for example Load/Unload Allowance, Additional SSF), Amount, note.
  4. Submit. It appears on that month's slips. Cancel it to take everything back.
Do not type here

Tea, meals, overtime and late fine — the system works these out. Typing them here pays them twice.

Income tax

The system works out tax on every slip. Sometimes accounts knows a better figure — then you change it.

  1. Open the person's Salary Slip (while it is still a Draft).
  2. Scroll to the Income Tax section. Computed Income Tax shows the system's figure.
  3. Tick Override Income Tax and type the correct amount in Income Tax (manual). Typing 0 means no tax this month.
  4. Save. Net pay changes by itself. Untick to go back to the system figure.

Dashain bonus once a year

One month's pay for a full year worked. Less for people who joined during the year.

  1. Open Dashain Bonus → + Add.
  2. Fill Company, Fiscal Year and Payout Date (a date in the month it will be paid).
  3. Not giving a bonus this year? Untick Bonus Given This Year, write the Reason, and Submit. Done.
  4. Giving it: choose One Month Means (usually Basic) and keep Percent of One Month at 100.
  5. Click Get Employees. Check the list — you can change any amount.
  6. Submit. It goes on that month's salary.

Example: joined 2.6 months before Dashain, Basic 20,000 → 20,000 × 2.6 ÷ 12 = Rs 4,333.

Salary increase once a year

Never type a new Basic on the old record. Always use Salary Revision — it keeps the history safe.

  1. Open Salary Revision → + Add.
  2. Fill Company and Effective From — always the first day of a BS month.
  3. Type the Hike % for Everyone (for example 10) and click Get Employees.
  4. Change any row: type a Hike % or a New Basic — the other fills itself. Change New DA if dearness allowance also rises.
  5. Raise decided late (for example, starts Shrawan but agreed in Mangsir)? Tick Pay Arrears and choose Arrears Paid In. The months already paid at the old rate are paid as arrears.
  6. Submit.

When something goes wrong

Click a problem to see why it happens and what to do.

A person is missing from the Payroll Entry

Why: they have no submitted Salary Structure Assignment, or it starts after this month.

Fix: make one (see new employee, step 5), then click Get Employees again.

Someone's salary slip shows 0 days or very low pay

Why: their attendance for the month is missing or marked Absent.

Fix: fix the attendance, click Prepare Payroll Inputs again, then open the slip and click Save.

Overtime is not on the slip

Why: no submitted Overtime Sheet for that day, the sheet was not measured, or the person is Officer & Admin.

Fix: submit the sheet, click Measure from Attendance, then Prepare Payroll Inputs again.

No tea money for someone

Why: their Allowance Category is empty or set to NO.

Fix: set the right tea group on the Employee, then Prepare Payroll Inputs again.

A manager got a late fine

Fix: tick Late Fine Exempt on their Employee and run Prepare Payroll Inputs again.

The advance was not deducted

Why: the cash payout voucher (Bank/Cash Entry) was never submitted, or Recover From is a later month.

Fix: submit the payout voucher, then Prepare Payroll Inputs again.

“Submit Salary Slip” fails with an account error

Why: the salary lines are not yet linked to accounts in the books.

Fix: stop and call the accountant or the system team. Do not try other buttons.

Late time looks wrong for one day

Why: the person was on a different shift that day, but no Shift Request was entered.

Fix: enter the Shift Request for that day (see Shifts). Then Cancel and Amend that day's Attendance so it re-measures.

Golden rule

If you are unsure, Save but do not Submit. A draft can always be fixed.

Part D — Every flow, start to finish

Each flow shows the screens in the order the work moves through them. Read left to right, top to bottom.

Joining a new person

Job Requisition→Job Opening→Job Applicant→Interview→Job Offer→Employee→Shift Assignment→Leave Policy Assignment→Salary Structure Assignment

Hiring screens (first five) are optional — most companies start at Employee. The last four are required, in that order, or the person is missing from attendance, leave or salary.

From finger punch to attendance

Biometric Device→Employee Checkin→Shift Type (every hour)→Attendance→Attendance Fix / Repair from Check-ins→Monthly Attendance BS

The machine sends punches; each hour the shift turns punches into attendance, measuring late and early minutes against that day's shift. More than 2 hours late becomes a Half Day. If the machine was offline, Attendance Fix rebuilds the days.

Changing a shift

Shift Request→Approved + Submit→Shift Assignment (split)→Attendance measured on new shift→Old shift returns after To Date

With no To Date the move is permanent and the old shift does not come back.

Leave, from policy to payslip

Leave Type→Leave Period→Leave Policy→Leave Control Panel→Leave Policy Assignment→Leave Allocation (+2.75 each BS month)→Leave Application→Leave Ledger Entry→Attendance: On Leave→Leave Encashment (year end)

Unpaid leave (Leave Without Pay) cuts that day's pay on the slip. Working while on leave turns the day into a Half Day.

Overtime and replacement leave

Overtime Sheet (on the day)→Measure from Attendance→Prepare Payroll Inputs→Additional Salary: Overtime→Salary Slip

Only for Plant staff. Officer & Admin staff who work a holiday get it back as leave — it shows in Yearly Leave Details BS.

An advance, from cash to paid back

Employee Advance→Create › Payment (Bank/Cash voucher)→Prepare Payroll Inputs each month→Additional Salary: Salary Advance→Salary Slip→Advance status: Returned

Each month takes the instalment or what is left, whichever is smaller.

Month-end salary

Attendance complete→Overtime Sheets measured→Payroll Adjustment / Dashain Bonus / Salary Revision→Payroll Entry (Get Employees, Save)→Prepare Payroll Inputs→Create Salary Slips→Check slips, override tax→Submit Salary Slip→Accounts voucher→Make Bank Entry

Prepare Payroll Inputs must come before Create Salary Slips — creating slips submits the Payroll Entry.

How tax lands on a slip

Payroll Period→Income Tax Slab→Salary Structure Assignment→Salary Slip: Computed Income Tax→Override Income Tax (if needed)

SSF members skip the first 1% (SST). Extra pay each month is taxed in that same month.

Once a year

New Fiscal Year (7 companies)→Holiday Lists + festivals→Leave Period→Leave Control Panel→Payroll Period→Income Tax Slab (new budget)→New Salary Structure if rates change→Salary Revision (raises)→Dashain Bonus→Leave Encashment

Do these before Shrawan 1, or documents in the new year cannot be saved.

A person leaves

Employee Separation→Exit Interview→Employee: Relieving Date + Status Left→Last Payroll Entry→Full and Final Statement→Create Journal Entry

The Full and Final Statement collects the last salary, unused leave, any advance still owed, and gratuity if it applies.
