How to run this plan

  1. Work top to bottom. Section A proves the setup; everything after it assumes A passed.
  2. For each case: follow Do, then check Expect, then look where Verify says.
  3. A case passes only if every line under Expect is true. Partly right is a fail.
  4. Record: case number, what you saw, a screenshot, and the record name (e.g. NGI-SAL-83/84-00012).
Reading the marks

Button is a button. Field is a box you type in. Screen Name is what you type in the search bar at the top.

Money rules, for checking by hand

Overtime per hour = Basic ÷ 30 ÷ 8 × 1.5 · Late fine = late minutes × Basic ÷ 30 ÷ 8 ÷ 60 · Tea = days present × the group's rate · Meal = meals × the group's rate · SSF = 31% of Basic, with 20% added as pay.

Before you begin

Ask for a backup of the test site. Several cases submit salary and post to the accounts; you will want to go back.

A · Setup is correct

Read-only checks. If one fails, stop and report it — later sections will fail for the wrong reason.

A1 · Fiscal year and periods

Do: open Fiscal Year → 83/84. Then Payroll Period and Leave Period.

Expect: fiscal year 2026-07-17 → 2027-07-16 with a row for all 7 companies · one payroll period and one leave period per company, same dates.

Why it matters: no payroll period means income tax cannot be worked out at all.

A2 · Holiday lists

Do: open Holiday List. Open each company's normal list and its Women list.

Expect: 52 Saturdays + 10 festivals (Janai Purnima, Fulpati, Maha Ashtami, Maha Nawami, Vijaya Dashami, Ekadashi, Laxmi Puja, Gobardhan Puja, Bhai Tika, Maghe Sankranti) · the Women list has 11 — the same plus Haritalika Teej, 2026-09-14 · no day appears twice.

Verify: a woman's Employee → Attendance & Leaves → Holiday List is the Women list; a man's is blank (he inherits the company's).

A3 · Shifts

Do: open Shift Type, then any one of them.

Expect, on every shift: Enable Auto Attendance ticked · Determine Check-in and Check-out = Alternating entries · Working Hours Calculation = First Check-in and Last Check-out · begin check-in before 120 · allow check-out after 120 · half day under 5 h · absent under 2 h · Late Entry Grace Period = 0 · Half Day If Late By (Hours) = 2 · Late Arrival Cutoff empty.

Watch this one

If Late Arrival Cutoff holds a time nobody typed, fail A3. It once filled itself with the time the shift was saved and turned 2,423 of 2,808 days into half days.

A4 · Payroll settings

Do: open Payroll Settings and Nepal HRMS Settings.

Expect: Calculate Payroll Working Days Based On = Attendance · Fraction of Daily Salary for Half Day = 0.5 · Process Payroll in Nepali Month ticked.

Note: Consider Unmarked Attendance As is Present today — a day with no attendance is paid. Confirm the client wants that.

A5 · Salary components

Do: open Salary Component.

Expect: 21 components · Income Tax has Variable Based On Taxable Salary ticked · SSF has Exempted from Income Tax ticked · SSF and SSF Addition both have Depends on Payment Days unticked · each component has an Accounts row for every company.

Why: if SSF had payment days ticked its amount would be cut twice for absence. With no Accounts row, slips cannot be submitted.

A6 · Structures and assignments

Do: open Salary Structure → NGI Staff 83/84, then Salary Structure Assignment for any NGI employee.

Expect: structure is submitted · HRA reads custom_initial_basic * 0.25 if custom_hra_eligible else 0 · assignment has From Date 2026-07-17, a Base, an Income Tax Slab and a Payroll Payable Account.

Why: an assignment with no payable account is silently left out of every payroll run.

A7 · Tax slab

Do: open Income Tax Slab → Nepal 83/84 - NGI.

Expect: submitted, effective 2026-07-17, Allow Tax Exemption ticked, and five rows — 0–10,00,000 at 1% with condition not custom_ssf_applicable, then 10%, 20%, 27%, 29%.

Why: that condition is the SST waiver for SSF members. Without it every SSF member pays 1% they should not.

B · Shifts and shift change

B1 · A temporary shift change

Do: pick an employee on the 6 AM shift. Shift Request → + Add → employee, Shift Type = the 12 PM shift, From Date = a Monday, To Date = that Wednesday, Status = Approved → Submit.

Expect: it submits without the error "already has an active Shift Assignment" · in Shift Assignment that person now has three rows — old shift ending the Sunday, the new shift Mon–Wed, the old shift again from Thursday, open-ended.

Verify: Monthly Attendance BS for that month — the Shift column shows the new shift on exactly those three days.

B2 · Cancelling gives the days back

Do: Cancel the request from B1.

Expect: the three rows collapse back to one open-ended assignment on the original shift.

B3 · A permanent move

Do: same as B1 but leave To Date empty.

Expect: the old assignment ends the day before and is not resumed. Two rows only.

B4 · Same shift is refused

Do: raise a request for the shift the person is already on.

Expect: refused with a message saying they are already on it.

B5 · Late time follows the new shift

Do: after B3, add a punch at 09:08 on a day inside the new 12 PM shift (Employee Checkin), then rebuild that day's attendance.

Expect: late = 0 — 09:08 is early for a 12 PM shift. Under the old 9 AM shift the same punch was 8 minutes late.

C · Attendance rules

For each case add punches in Employee Checkin (log type IN then OUT), then let the shift build attendance — Shift Type → Mark Attendance — or use Attendance Fix.

C1 · A normal day

Do: 9 AM shift, IN 08:58, OUT 17:05.

Expect: status Present · late 0 · Late Time on the report 0.

C2 · A little late

Do: 9 AM shift, IN 09:40, OUT 17:30.

Expect: status Present (not half day — under two hours) · late 40 minutes, stored on the attendance row and shown in the report.

C3 · More than two hours late

Do: 9 AM shift, IN 11:15, OUT 18:00.

Expect: status Half Day, whatever the hours add up to · leave type on the row = Leave Without Pay · no late fine for that day (the half day is the penalty).

Verify on the slip: that day counts 0.5 in payment days.

C4 · Too few hours

Do: IN 09:00, OUT 12:30 (3.5 hours).

Expect: Half Day (under 5 hours). Under 2 hours it would be Absent.

C5 · No punch at all

Do: leave a working day with no punches, then run the shift's Mark Attendance.

Expect: Absent · that day is not paid · it appears as Absent in Monthly Attendance BS.

C6 · Came in while on leave

Do: approve a leave for a day, then add punches on that day.

Expect: status becomes Half Day with the leave type kept — half worked, half leave.

C7 · Worked on a holiday

Do: add punches on a Saturday or a festival day, then run Attendance Fix for that date.

Expect: attendance is created with Worked on Holiday ticked.

Known behaviour

The automatic run skips holidays, so holiday work only appears after Attendance Fix. If it appeared by itself, note it — the behaviour changed.

C8 · Machine was offline

Do: Attendance Fix → shift, company, a date range with missing days → Submit.

Expect: days with punches become Present/Half Day · days with none become Absent · holidays are skipped · attendance already entered by hand is left alone · the Per-Day Log lists what it did.

D · Leave

D1 · Leave grows each BS month

Do: open Leave Allocation for one NGI and one NGK employee.

Expect: NGI casual grows 1.75 a month and sick 1.0 (21 and 12 a year) · NGK casual 0.67, sick 1.0 (8 and 12) · credited on the last day of each BS month, not the 1st of an English month.

D2 · Taking leave

Do: Leave Application → employee, Casual Leave, two days inside the test month, Status Approved → Submit.

Expect: balance falls by 2 · those days show On Leave in Monthly Attendance BS · a Leave Ledger Entry exists.

D3 · Unpaid leave costs pay

Do: apply Leave Without Pay for 2 days, then rebuild that person's slip.

Expect: payment days fall by 2 · Basic and the allowances fall in proportion · SSF, being 31% of Basic, falls with it.

D4 · The year's ceiling

Do: open a casual allocation → Allocate Leaves → add 4 days. Then try adding 25.

Expect: 4 is accepted (the month's amount is free) · 25 is refused: "cannot exceed annual allocation of 21".

D5 · Maternity and paternity

Do: apply Maternity Leave for a male employee, and Paternity Leave for a female one.

Expect: both refused — the system reads the employee's Gender.

E · Overtime

E1 · Overtime needs a sheet

Do: give a Plant / Operation employee punches two hours past their shift end, build attendance, run the month's Prepare Payroll Inputs — but create no Overtime Sheet.

Expect: no overtime is paid. The hours show in the report's O.T. column, but nothing reaches the slip.

E2 · An authorised sheet pays

Do: Overtime Sheet → company, that date, a reason, add the employee → Submit → Measure from Attendance. Then Prepare Payroll Inputs again.

Expect: Worked Hours (measured) ≈ 2, rounded to the nearest half hour · an Additional Salary for Overtime appears · amount = hours × Basic ÷ 30 ÷ 8 × 1.5. Check it by hand.

E3 · Officers earn leave, not overtime

Do: put an Admin & Officer employee on an Overtime Sheet for a holiday they worked.

Expect: the row says Replacement Leave, not Overtime · no overtime money on the slip · the day counts in Work On Holiday BS and is credited back in Yearly Leave Details BS.

E4 · Holiday hours count in full

Do: an Operation employee works 7 h 53 m on a holiday; sheet it and measure.

Expect: 8.0 hours — every hour counts on a holiday, rounded to the nearest half hour. On a working day only the hours outside the shift count.

F · Tea, meals and the late fine

F1 · Tea follows the group

Do: take one NGI OLD and one NGI NEW employee with the same days present. Run Prepare Payroll Inputs.

Expect: OLD = days × 235 · NEW = days × 40 · someone in NO gets nothing · someone with no group gets nothing.

F2 · A half day still earns full tea

Do: find a person with a Half Day in the month.

Expect: that day counts as a whole day of tea — turning up earns it.

F3 · Meals

Do: one day IN 1½ h before the shift and OUT 1½ h after; another day only 1 h early.

Expect: first day 2 meals · second day 0 · never more than 2 a day · on a holiday, 6 hours worked = 1 meal, 8 hours = 2.

F4 · Late fine

Do: a person 38 minutes late on a Basic of 17,860.

Expect: fine = 38 × 17,860 ÷ 30 ÷ 8 ÷ 60 = 47.13 · it appears as a deduction on the slip.

F5 · Managers are exempt

Do: tick Late Fine Exempt on an Employee, run Prepare Payroll Inputs again.

Expect: no Late Fine for that person, however late they were. Late minutes still show in the report.

F6 · Re-running does not double up

Do: press Prepare Payroll Inputs three times.

Expect: amounts are the same each time — the old rows are replaced, not added to.

G · Advances

G1 · Give an advance

Do: Employee Advance → employee, Advance Amount 10,000, purpose, Repay Unclaimed Amount from Salary ticked, Monthly Instalment 2,500 → Submit → Make → Bank Entry → choose a JV Type → Submit.

Expect: advance status Paid · the voucher debits Staff Advance.

G2 · It comes back monthly

Do: run Prepare Payroll Inputs for the month.

Expect: a Salary Advance deduction of 2,500 on the slip · the advance shows 7,500 still owed.

G3 · The last instalment

Do: on an advance with 1,000 left and an instalment of 2,500, run the month.

Expect: it deducts 1,000, not 2,500 · status becomes Returned.

G4 · Never paid out, never deducted

Do: submit an advance but skip the Bank Entry. Run the month.

Expect: nothing is deducted — the system only takes back money it knows was handed over.

H · The monthly run

H1 · BS dates fill themselves

Do: Payroll Entry → company, Payroll Frequency Monthly, Posting Date any day inside a BS month.

Expect: start and end dates jump to that BS month (e.g. 2026-08-17 → 2026-09-16), not the English month.

H2 · Order matters

Do: Get Employees → Save → Nepal HRMS → Prepare Payroll Inputs → Create Salary Slips.

Expect: the inputs message reports rows created · slips carry tea, meal, overtime and fines.

Try it the wrong way too

Create Salary Slips before Prepare Payroll Inputs: the slips will have no tea or meals. Fix = run the inputs, then open each slip and Save. Confirm that works.

H3 · Days paid

Do: open a slip for someone with 2 absences and 1 half day in a 31-day BS month.

Expect: total working days 31 · payment days 28.5 · Basic and allowances prorated to that.

H4 · Against the client's sheet

Do: take five NGI employees and compare their slip line by line with the client's Falgun sheet (full month, nobody absent).

Expect: Basic, HRA, gas, education, dearness, other, SSF Addition and SSF match to the paisa.

H5 · Nobody is missing

Do: compare the count on the Payroll Entry with active employees who have a salary assignment.

Expect: the same number. Anyone missing has no assignment, or an assignment with no Payroll Payable Account.

I · Income tax

I1 · SSF members skip the 1%

Do: compare a person in the SSF earning under ten lakh a year with one outside it.

Expect: the SSF member pays 0 · the non-member pays 1% of their pay (that 1% is the SST).

I2 · A higher earner

Do: open the slip of someone whose yearly pay passes ten lakh.

Expect: tax appears · the yearly figure is after SSF is deducted (SSF reduces taxable pay).

I3 · Overriding tax

Do: on a draft slip, tick Override Income Tax, type 7,500, Save. Then type 0 and Save. Then untick and Save.

Expect: deduction becomes 7,500, then 0, then back to the computed figure · net pay and the amount in words follow each time · Computed Income Tax keeps showing the system's own number throughout.

I4 · Extra pay is taxed in its month

Do: add a one-off earning (say a 20,000 bonus) and rebuild the slip.

Expect: tax rises in that month, not spread thinly over the rest of the year.

J · Posting to the books

J1 · Submitting writes one journal

Do: on the Payroll Entry, Submit Salary Slip.

Expect: every slip is submitted · one Journal Entry exists · debit equals credit · it carries a JV Type and a Document No without anyone typing them.

J2 · Each section to its own account

Do: open that journal.

Expect: office pay on 547101 O/O, sales on 547102 S/D, plant on 547103 F/P, each with its own cost centre · SSF on 347302 · tax on 348102 · Salary Payable credited with exactly the month's net pay.

J3 · Paying out

Do: Make Bank Entry on the Payroll Entry.

Expect: a voucher clearing Salary Payable against the bank, for the net amount.

J4 · Undoing a month

Do: cancel the journal, then the slips.

Expect: it can be done in that order, and the accounts return to where they were. Report anything that blocks it.

K · One-offs, bonus and raises

K1 · Payroll Adjustment

Do: Payroll Adjustment → company, a payroll date inside the month, rows for two people (one earning, one deduction) → Submit. Rebuild their slips.

Expect: both amounts appear on the right slips · cancelling the adjustment removes them.

K2 · Dashain bonus, full year

Do: Dashain Bonus → company, fiscal year, payout date, One Month Means = Basic, 100% → Get Employees.

Expect: anyone with a year or more shows 12.00 months and a bonus equal to one month's Basic.

K3 · Dashain bonus, mid-year joiner

Do: look at someone who joined part-way through.

Expect: months served under 12 and the bonus prorated — e.g. 2.6 months on 20,000 = 4,333 · every amount stays editable · Submit puts it on that month's slip.

K4 · Bonus not given

Do: untick Bonus Given This Year, write a reason, Submit.

Expect: it saves as a record of the decision · nobody is paid.

K5 · A pay rise

Do: Salary Revision → company, Effective From = the first day of a BS month, Hike % for Everyone = 10 → Get Employees → Submit.

Expect: each person gets a new Salary Structure Assignment from that date · the old one is untouched · next month's slip uses the new Basic, last month's is unchanged.

K6 · Arrears for a late rise

Do: repeat K5 with Effective From in a month already paid, tick Pay Arrears, choose the month to pay them in.

Expect: arrears = the monthly difference × months already paid · they appear as a Salary Arrears earning in the chosen month · submitted slips are never edited.

L · Reports

L1 · Monthly Attendance BS — Detail

Expect: one row per person per day · the Shift of that day · Late minutes counted from that shift's start, with no grace · Before ofc. Time for leaving early · O.T. hours for Operation staff only · Meal and Tea quantities · holidays named in Remarks.

Cross-check: Late on a day matches the punch (09:08 on a 9 AM shift = 8).

L2 · Monthly Attendance BS — Summary

Expect: one row per person · Late Time = late + left early · Present Days counting a half day as 0.5 · Leave this month, last month and to date · these totals equal the Detail view's for the same person.

L3 · Yearly Leave Details BS

Expect: leave taken by BS month · entitlement so far · Leave Remaining · for Admin & Officer staff, holidays worked are credited back, but never above the year's entitlement.

L4 · Work On Holiday BS

Expect: holidays worked per person per BS month, with their OT eligibility · matches the Worked on Holiday flags in Detail.

L5 · Salary Register

Expect: every slip and line for the month · the totals equal the Payroll Entry's and the journal's.

Known limits — not defects

  • Auto attendance skips holidays. Holiday work appears only after Attendance Fix.
  • A slip is paid at one rate for the whole month. A pay rise dated mid-month takes effect the following month.
  • Unmarked days are treated as Present under the current Payroll Settings — a missing punch is paid.
  • Submitting a Payroll Entry with more than 30 employees queues slip creation to a background worker; if the queue is down, the entry sits at "Queued" and no slips appear.
  • Tea, meal and overtime are not in the salary structure. They arrive as Additional Salary each month, so they are absent from a slip built before Prepare Payroll Inputs was run.
  • Employee fields are current, not historical. Changing someone's allowance group today does not alter a submitted slip, but it does change what the next run pays.
Reporting a defect

Give the case number, the record name, what you expected, what you saw, and the exact figures. A slip or journal name is enough for anyone to reproduce it.
