The PDF this template produces is already correct (wider than tall, /Rotate 0), so a print that comes out sideways or upside down was turned by the DRIVER or the PDF viewer, downstream of everything here. Rather than chase that machine by machine, turn the content to cancel it out: rot=0 (default) the form as-is, 241.3 x 139.7mm rot=90 a quarter turn clockwise into a PORTRAIT page — for a driver locked to portrait (the usual cause of a "sideways" print) and for a printer fed the short way rot=180 same page, upside down — for a form fed in reverse rot=270 the other quarter turn The page box is swapped for 90/270 so the sheet always matches what is drawn on it, leaving nothing downstream any reason to rotate again.
